# Advanced Intelligence & Applied Computing (AIAC)

### AIAC Research Group

The **Advanced Intelligence & Applied Computing (AIAC) Research Group** is a
research community within the **Department of Computer Science & Engineering,
BMS Institute of Technology & Management**.

AIAC brings together motivated student researchers to explore Artificial
Intelligence, Machine Learning, and emerging computing technologies through
hands-on experimentation, collaborative engineering, and research-driven
projects. The group focuses on developing practical, reproducible, and
industry-oriented solutions while building strong research and engineering
capabilities.

---

## Research Areas

Artificial Intelligence · Machine Learning · Natural Language Processing ·
Computer Vision · Agentic AI · Healthcare & Bio-Signals · Secure AI ·
High-Performance Computing & MLOps

---

## Research Pods

AIAC operates through **6 specialized research pods**, each focused on a
distinct research domain.

### Pod 1 — Regional NLP & Indic LLMs
Kannada text classification, pseudo-labeling, and LoRA-based adaptation.

### Pod 2 — Edge AI & Vision Systems
Real-time computer vision and intelligent transportation systems using
technologies such as YOLO and TensorRT.

### Pod 3 — Agentic AI & Autonomous Loops
Multi-agent reasoning, autonomous workflows, and grounded Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG).

### Pod 4 — Healthcare & Bio-Signals
ECG/EEG time-series analysis and anomaly detection using healthcare research
datasets.

### Pod 5 — Secure AI & Model Ethics
Adversarial robustness, fairness, model security, and jailbreak research.

### Pod 6 — Systems, HPC & MLOps
GPU infrastructure, automated testing, CI/CD, and reproducible ML systems.

---

## Our Approach

We emphasize **collaborative research, reproducibility, peer review,

into reliable intelligent systems.
